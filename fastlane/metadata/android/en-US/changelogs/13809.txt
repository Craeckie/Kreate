## 🐛 Fixes
- 🎵 **Hard-to-play songs now play** — songs that used to start, play for a few seconds, then go silent (or get stuck swapping between two unavailable versions) now fall back to a lower-quality stream that plays all the way through
- 🔁 **No more rematch ping-pong** — auto-rematch no longer bounces back and forth between two unavailable uploads of the same song
