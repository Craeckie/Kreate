## 🐛 Fixes
- 🔑 **YouTube login** — sign-in now captures your session reliably, and a half-loaded page can no longer wipe the credentials you already had
- 🔄 **YouTube sync** — albums, artists and playlists sync correctly again
- 💥 **Home screen crash** — fixed a crash when an album or artist appeared both in your library and in the sync result
- ▶️ **Playback** — fixed PO-token generation that could never succeed, which left some songs refusing to play
- 🖼️ Fixed the playback crash "cannot use a recycled source in createBitmap"
- 📥 **Downloads** — the download badge and its tap now agree on what is downloaded, deletions always go through the download manager, and downloaded songs are no longer silently evicted
- 🎵 Tapping a song in search results now plays the whole playlist, not just the results
- 🗒️ Crash logs are cleaned up when you dismiss the crash dialog

## 🎨 Visual
- 🖼️ Consistent artist thumbnail size in Quick Picks
- 📉 Shorter, filtered error messages

## ⚠️ Known Change
- 📴 Synced YouTube albums and artists are now shown **online only** — they are no longer kept on the device, so they will not appear while offline. Your own bookmarked albums and artists are unaffected.

## 🔧 Changes
- ⚡ Each stream URL is resolved once instead of once per caller
- 📼 Video tracks are no longer selected during playback — less data used for audio-only listening
- 🌍 Translation updates
- 🧱 Rebased on upstream Kreate 2.2.3
